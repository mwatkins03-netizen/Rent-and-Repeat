About this prototype

Rent & Repeat

The idea. Ole Miss students who rent out dresses and outfits for date parties, formals, swaps, Grove game days and Square nights currently run everything through Instagram DMs: prices, sizes, availability, who has what and when it comes back. Rent & Repeat keeps all of that in one place and only ever shows renters what is actually free for their dates.

Version one (this build): event categories, outfit listings, an availability calendar, rental requests, an owner closet, and a bookings desk that prevents overlaps.

Preview of later features: a numbers page computed from real bookings, with a manual field for Instagram views. Saved for later: payments, return reminders, renter reviews, search near campus, the real Instagram API.

Your data

Everything you type, including photos, is saved in this browser only (localStorage). Nothing is sent anywhere. Clearing your browser data erases it, so back up before you do.

Content: outfit photos are the owner's own uploads. The drawn outfits you see before any photo is added are generated illustrations, not real clothes. No real people appear in the app. Pickup spots are familiar Oxford landmarks used as labels only.
